About

PS

I build enterprise-grade user interfaces for cloud-native platforms — the dashboards, consoles, and tooling that help teams manage Kubernetes clusters, CI/CD pipelines, and AI/ML workloads at scale.

My work spans the Red Hat and open-source ecosystem, from OpenShift Console to Tekton Pipelines to Open Data Hub. I focus on making complex infrastructure accessible, intuitive, and beautiful through thoughtful design systems and component architecture.

I specialize in React, TypeScript, and enterprise design systems like PatternFly and Carbon, building scalable frontends that serve millions of developers and operators worldwide.
